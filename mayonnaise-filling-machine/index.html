The difference between a workable mayonnaise filling machine and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for seasoning and sauce packers, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a mayonnaise filling machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 5258–6758 units per hour for 2 L product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

A mayonnaise filling machine is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±1.5 ml, 5258–6758 units per hour, 16–23 kW, and 3200 × 1400 × 2400 mm, on 2 L product.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a mayonnaise filling machine as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 3200 mm working width, it becomes part of the seasoning and sauce packers control loop, and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A mayonnaise filling machine with a 7-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a seasoning and sauce packers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this mayonnaise filling machine the wear items are simple to identify — seals, belt, guide rails — and most seasoning and sauce packers downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the mayonnaise filling machine?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 5258 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Can the mayonnaise filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 5258–6758 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 2 L range before ordering.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a seasoning and sauce packers operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
