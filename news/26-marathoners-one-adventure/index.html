26 Marathoners, one Adventure

They work at KIWL Machine, they're determined, motivated... and a bit crazy 😄- On April 13, they'll be setting off on the Paris Marathon. For most of them, it will be a first!

They work at KIWL Machine, and they’re determined, motivated… and a bit crazy
On April 13, they’ll be taking part in the Marathon de Paris. For most of them, it will be a first!

-

There are 26 of them. 26 KIWL Machine employees, 26 destinies united under a single banner, with a single goal: to cross the finish line of the Marathon de Paris. Among them are regular runners and running enthusiasts, as well as novices who would never have imagined taking on such a challenge.

Apprentices, engineers, R&D, HR, sales, … They come from different backgrounds, but on April 13, 2025 they will all form a single team. A larger team than in 2023, with 10 more runners, all driven by the same desire to surpass themselves. But before the big day, there’s the training, the sweat, the doubts and the personal victories. We followed some of them in their final preparations, sharing their efforts, their moments of doubt, and their progress. Soon comes the race, 42.195 km of challenge, fatigue, but above all, emotion. From the cobbled streets of Paris to the Champs-Élysées, every stride will tell a story.

And we’ll be there to bring it to life. 🏃‍♂️ Who will keep up? Who will realize their dream?
