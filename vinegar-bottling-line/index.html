Vinegar Bottling Line

Published 2026-11-21 · KIWL Machine

A vinegar bottling line sits on the critical path of the seasoning and sauce packers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a vinegar bottling line.

What This Equipment Does

A vinegar bottling line is the metering station that decides the dose every seasoning and sauce packers container receives. The reference build delivers 5743–7243 bottles per hour at ±0.5 ml deviation, and accepts 16 oz containers on a common neck finish.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a vinegar bottling line on the specification that limits your output: the 0.5 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 10-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The vinegar bottling line is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the seasoning and sauce packers workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A vinegar bottling line rated at 5743–7243 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the vinegar bottling line leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the vinegar bottling line?

The reference build draws 11–16 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

What spare parts should be held for the vinegar bottling line?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 5743 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
