Soy Sauce Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-22 · KIWL Machine

Before any soy sauce filling machine quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for seasoning and sauce packers operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a soy sauce filling machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 3684–5184 units per hour for 12 oz product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

For a soy sauce filling machine aimed at seasoning and sauce packers operations, the practical reference build is SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts, a 7-inch colour touch and a 15–20 kW connected load inside 1600 × 1900 × 2800 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This soy sauce filling machine takes a standard encoder signal and a 1600 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the soy sauce filling machine against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 3684–5184 bottles per hour a typical seasoning and sauce packers operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a soy sauce filling machine because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 7-inch colour touch that records the last 15 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the soy sauce filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 3684–5184 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 12 oz range before ordering.

Does the soy sauce filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1900 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

Can the soy sauce filling machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 12 oz product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
