Our expertise

3,000

Number of products  tested in our laboratories. For each product, the filling characteristics are evaluated, then saved in our database. Product viscosity, foaming capacity, homogeneity, inflammability, aggressiveness, … combined with the container characteristics and required work-rate, all of which have an influence on the filling time and consequently determine the configuration of your machine.

Filling of viscous, foaming, abrasive or corrosive products, …

Whether your products are sensitive to the effects of shearing, viscosity, foaming, abrasive or corrosive, we provide filling solutions specifically adapted to your needs in order to guarantee optimum performance when filling your products.

A wide variety of capping solutions

The wide variety of caps and capping techniques proposed on today’s market has led us to develop ever-more flexible and versatile capping systems.

Our machines produce your PET containers

The preferential heating technology used on our blowing machines enables optimum distribution of the raw material whether forming oval or asymmetric containers.
