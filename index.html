About Jiangsu KIWL Machinery Manufacturing Group Co., Ltd

Established in 2012, Jiangsu KIWL Machinery Manufacturing Group Co., Ltd is a reputable China leading manufacturer focused on customized designing, manufacturing and service of liquid bottling equipment.

Our main equipment includes liquid fillers, bottle cappers, labeling applicators, bottle rinsers, unscramblers, sorters, and conveyor systems. We provide one-stop / turnkey bottling solutions for daily chemical, cosmetics, food sauce & oil industries worldwide.

Factory location: Building 4, Xingyuan Road, Nanfeng Town, Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, China.
