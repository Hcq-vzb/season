Jam Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-26 · KIWL Machine

Most disagreements about a jam filling machine come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the seasoning and sauce packers requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 5670–7170 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what happens after the jam filling machine: the container leaves at 5670–7170 bottles per hour with a deviation of ±2.0 ml, and every downstream step inherits that number. A tight tolerance is what keeps capping, labelling and inspection settings valid for a full run.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 5670–7170 units per hour means little without 330 ml can product, a 2.0 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover — all three stated for this jam filling machine.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The jam filling machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the seasoning and sauce packers workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the jam filling machine to real 330 ml can volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 5670–7170 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a jam filling machine is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 5670–7170 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this jam filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±2.0 ml on 330 ml can containers at 5670–7170 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Does the jam filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1400 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
