Black Pepper Packing Machine

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most black pepper packing machine purchases, and they are the same for every seasoning and sauce packers operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

What a black pepper packing machine does to the seasoning and sauce packers production line is measurable at the packing end. At 2196–3196 units per hour for 500 ml can product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a black pepper packing machine specification for what is measurable: rate 2196–3196 units per hour, deviation ±3.0 ml, container 500 ml can, control 10-inch colour touch, load 3–4 kW, footprint 1600 × 3600 × 2100 mm, changeover 10 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the black pepper packing machine determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 2196–3196 units per hour and a 10-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A black pepper packing machine handling 2196–3196 units per hour with a 3.0 ml tolerance attacks all three for a seasoning and sauce packers operation.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a black pepper packing machine is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 2196–3196 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

What accuracy can this black pepper packing machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 3.0 ml on 500 ml can product at 2196–3196 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the black pepper packing machine handle a 500 ml can product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
