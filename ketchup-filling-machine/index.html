Ketchup Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-14 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a ketchup filling machine. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the seasoning and sauce packers production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

Position a ketchup filling machine by what it removes from the seasoning and sauce packers process. This configuration handles 250–500 ml product at 1710–2360 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this ketchup filling machine the tolerance is 3.0 ml, the contact parts are SUS304 with sanitary fittings, and the controls run on a 7-inch colour touch.

Integration With Your Line

Shared cleaning and shared controls are what make a ketchup filling machine economical. When it speaks the same protocol as the rest of the seasoning and sauce packers production line, a recipe change reaches it without a person walking to the machine.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A ketchup filling machine at 1710–2360 bottles per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 250–500 ml containers, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the seasoning and sauce packers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this ketchup filling machine the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 20-minute routine service realistic on a 1710 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the ketchup filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 2800 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

Can the ketchup filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 1710–2360 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 250–500 ml range before ordering.

What accuracy can this ketchup filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±3.0 ml on 250–500 ml containers at 1710–2360 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
