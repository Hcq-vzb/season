Filling And Capping Monoblock

Published 2026-10-29 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a filling and capping monoblock. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the seasoning and sauce packers production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what happens after the filling and capping monoblock: the container leaves at 7181–10181 bottles per hour with a deviation of ±0.5 ml, and every downstream step inherits that number. A tight tolerance is what keeps capping, labelling and inspection settings valid for a full run.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a filling and capping monoblock on the specification that limits your output: the ±0.5 ml tolerance, the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts, and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often you have to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 3200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a filling and capping monoblock arrives on a seasoning and sauce packers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the filling and capping monoblock against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 7181–10181 units per hour a typical seasoning and sauce packers operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a filling and capping monoblock because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 10-inch colour touch that records the last 15 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the filling and capping monoblock?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 7181 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Does the filling and capping monoblock need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1400 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How long does a format changeover take on this filling and capping monoblock?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change on a new 1 L format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
