Pepper Sauce Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-27 · KIWL Machine

Choosing the right pepper sauce filling machine is a practical decision for seasoning and sauce packers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a pepper sauce filling machine on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

Dosing repeatability is the figure to insist on during FAT. A machine that holds ±0.5 ml on 5 L containers at 9905–13405 bottles per hour will keep the line within tolerance for the whole campaign, not only for the first hour.

Key Technical Specifications

Specification discipline for a pepper sauce filling machine means one page with: rate 9905–13405 units per hour, tolerance ±0.5 ml, container 5 L, control 7-inch colour touch, load 16–23 kW, footprint 3200 × 3600 × 2400 mm, changeover 20 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

A pepper sauce filling machine fits an existing seasoning and sauce packers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 3200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the pepper sauce filling machine against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 9905–13405 units per hour a typical seasoning and sauce packers operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a pepper sauce filling machine with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and 9905–13405 bottles per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the pepper sauce filling machine?

The reference build draws 16–23 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Does the pepper sauce filling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 3600 × 2400 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a seasoning and sauce packers operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
