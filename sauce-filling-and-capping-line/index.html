Sauce Filling And Capping Line

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machine

There is no universal sauce filling and capping line for seasoning and sauce packers. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

The reason to specify a sauce filling and capping line carefully is that the cost of a poor fit is paid over years. This configuration is built for 8 oz containers, 10074–13574 units per hour and a 1.5 ml tolerance, so the fit is decided before the order, not after commissioning.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not just the power total. This sauce filling and capping line draws 5.5–7.5 kW across a 1200 × 3600 × 3200 mm footprint with 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

A sauce filling and capping line that shares its control protocol with the rest of the seasoning and sauce packers production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A sauce filling and capping line handling 10074–13574 units per hour with a 1.5 ml tolerance attacks all three for a seasoning and sauce packers operation.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the sauce filling and capping line. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the sauce filling and capping line?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 10074 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Can the sauce filling and capping line be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 10074–13574 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 8 oz range before ordering.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a seasoning and sauce packers operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
