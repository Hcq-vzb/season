Capping Machine

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Machine

A capping machine for seasoning and sauce packers is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

Seam quality determines whether the pack survives handling. On this capping machine the torque window is 2.0–3 N·m, which keeps the double seam consistent at 5718–7218 cans per minute.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a capping machine against its torque control rather than its peak rate. A unit holding 2.0–3 N·m at 5718–7218 cans per minute with a 10-minute changeover is worth more to a seasoning and sauce packers operation than a faster unit that needs constant adjustment.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a capping machine can absorb. With 5718–7218 units per hour and a 10-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The best ROI argument for a capping machine is where the seasoning and sauce packers production line currently loses output during format changes. At 10 minutes and 5718–7218 cans per minute, that loss is measurable and recoverable.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the capping machine. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

What seam strength can this capping machine guarantee?

The servo head holds a 2.0–3 N·m torque window across 16 oz format at 5718–7218 cans per minute. Seam strength depends on the lid material as well, so test with production lids.

What causes most rejects on a capping machine?

In practice, worn seaming rollers and lid tracking issues account for most of it. Both are covered by the recommended service interval.

Does the capping machine need a dedicated operator?

No. Once the format recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, the changeover is a guided sequence, and the 10-minute routine replaces manual adjustment.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
