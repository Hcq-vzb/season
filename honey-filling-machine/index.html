Honey Filling Machine

Published 2026-11-16 · KIWL Machine

A honey filling machine sits on the critical path of the seasoning and sauce packers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, contact materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a honey filling machine.

What This Equipment Does

Where most seasoning and sauce packers production lines lose output is not the filler itself but the buffer feeding it. This honey filling machine is sized at 14205–18205 bottles per hour with a ±1.0 ml tolerance, which means it can absorb a short upstream stoppage without starving the downstream labeller.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification question for a honey filling machine is not whether it meets the target but what it gives up to meet it. This build meets 14205 units per hour on 2 L product while holding 1.0 ml and changing over in 12 minutes, using 316L stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

The honey filling machine should be indexed, not merely adjacent. This build takes a line encoder signal across a 3200 mm working width, which means the seasoning and sauce packers production line can treat it as a controlled step rather than a separate operation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on a honey filling machine: less material lost, fewer hours worked, less time stopped. At a 1.0 ml tolerance and 14205–18205 units per hour, all three move together for a seasoning and sauce packers operation.

Maintenance and Service

For a seasoning and sauce packers operation the maintenance question for a honey filling machine is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the honey filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 14205–18205 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 2 L range before ordering.

Can the honey filling machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 2 L product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
