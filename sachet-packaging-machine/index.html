Sachet Packaging Machine

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machine

Before any sachet packaging machine quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for seasoning and sauce packers operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

For a seasoning and sauce packers operation, the sachet packaging machine is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 6147–9147 units per hour on 20 L product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

For a seasoning and sauce packers buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: 304/316L stainless steel product-contact parts, 10-inch colour touch controls, 0.5 ml tolerance, 12-minute changeover, 33–48 kW connected load and a 1200 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a sachet packaging machine can absorb. With 6147–9147 units per hour and a 12-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the sachet packaging machine on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 304/316L stainless steel contact parts and a 0.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same seasoning and sauce packers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Availability planning for a sachet packaging machine starts with the assumption that a seal will need replacing in the next quarter. Holding the 304/316L stainless steel contact-part spares on site is what keeps a 12-minute repair a 12-minute repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the sachet packaging machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the sachet packaging machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

What accuracy can this sachet packaging machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 20 L product at 6147–9147 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the sachet packaging machine be upgraded later?

Within the 6147–9147 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 20 L range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
