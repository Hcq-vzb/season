Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this metal detector for seasoning line hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 12 oz product at 8565–11565 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

What does the metal detector for seasoning line do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

How do I size this for my plant?
