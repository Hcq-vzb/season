Spice Grinding Machine

Published 2026-11-09 · KIWL Machine

A spice grinding machine for seasoning and sauce packers is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a spice grinding machine removes from the seasoning and sauce packers process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 8 oz product at 2704–3704 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

This spice grinding machine draws 30–45 kW and occupies 1600 × 2800 × 2100 mm, uses 304 stainless steel contact parts and holds a 2.0 ml tolerance on 8 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

A spice grinding machine fits an existing seasoning and sauce packers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on a spice grinding machine: less material lost, fewer hours worked, less time stopped. At a 2.0 ml tolerance and 2704–3704 units per hour, all three move together for a seasoning and sauce packers operation.

Maintenance and Service

The spice grinding machine keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 2704 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the spice grinding machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 2800 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

Is the spice grinding machine suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 2704–3704 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the spice grinding machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The seasoning and sauce packers team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
