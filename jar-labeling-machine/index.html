Jar Labeling Machine

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most jar labeling machine purchases, and they are the same for every seasoning and sauce packers operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A jar labeling machine should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 500 ml product at 17853–22353 units per hour, which is the span most seasoning and sauce packers production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

A jar labeling machine is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 17853–22353 units per hour, contact parts SUS304 with sanitary fittings, control 10-inch colour touch, load 3–4 kW, footprint 1200 × 2800 × 2100 mm, changeover 20 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the jar labeling machine can hand over at 17853–22353 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A jar labeling machine handling 17853–22353 units per hour with a 2.0 ml tolerance attacks all three for a seasoning and sauce packers operation.

Maintenance and Service

The jar labeling machine keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 17853 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 500 ml product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What is the delivery lead time for the jar labeling machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 500 ml set is included.

What accuracy can this jar labeling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds 2.0 ml on 500 ml product at 17853–22353 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
