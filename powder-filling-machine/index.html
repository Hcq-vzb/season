Powder Filling Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a powder filling machine. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the seasoning and sauce packers production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

A powder filling machine earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1521–2171 units per hour for 500 ml can product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Specification discipline for a powder filling machine means one page with: rate 1521–2171 units per hour, tolerance ±1.5 ml, container 500 ml can, control 7-inch colour touch, load 7.5–10.5 kW, footprint 1200 × 2200 × 2800 mm, changeover 20 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This powder filling machine takes a standard encoder signal and a 1200 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the powder filling machine on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 316L stainless steel contact parts and a 1.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same seasoning and sauce packers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a powder filling machine is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 1521–2171 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the powder filling machine?

The reference build draws 7.5–10.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

What spare parts should be held for the powder filling machine?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 1521 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
