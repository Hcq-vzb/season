Seasoning Bottle Capping Machine

Published 2026-11-08 · KIWL Machine

Most disagreements about a seasoning bottle capping machine come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the seasoning and sauce packers requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 5367–6867 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

Position a seasoning bottle capping machine by what it removes from the seasoning and sauce packers process. This configuration handles 60–120 mm diameter product at 5367–6867 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a seasoning bottle capping machine contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 1.5 ml tolerance, the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact-part grade, the 36–51 kW load and the 1200 × 1900 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a seasoning bottle capping machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the seasoning and sauce packers production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A seasoning bottle capping machine with a 7-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a seasoning bottle capping machine with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and 5367–6867 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the neck finish verified?

With a neck ring gauge and a go/no-go plug on the finished bottle.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What bottle sizes can this line cover?

The reference configuration covers the 60–120 mm diameter format range; other sizes need a tooling change.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
