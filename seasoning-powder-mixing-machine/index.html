Seasoning Powder Mixing Machine

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machine

A seasoning powder mixing machine for seasoning and sauce packers is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a seasoning powder mixing machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 327 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the seasoning and sauce packers production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference seasoning powder mixing machine uses food-grade 304 stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 25–36 kW connected load and a 1600 × 1400 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A seasoning powder mixing machine fits an existing seasoning and sauce packers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the seasoning powder mixing machine on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and a 0.3 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same seasoning and sauce packers production line.

Maintenance and Service

The seasoning powder mixing machine keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 327 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the seasoning powder mixing machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 327 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How long does a format changeover take on this seasoning powder mixing machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 12 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
