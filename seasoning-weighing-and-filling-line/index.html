Seasoning Weighing And Filling Line

Published 2026-11-10 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a seasoning weighing and filling line for seasoning and sauce packers. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

Dosing repeatability is the figure to insist on during FAT. A machine that holds ±1.0 ml on 500 ml can containers at 8135–11635 bottles per hour will keep the line within tolerance for the whole campaign, not only for the first hour.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A seasoning weighing and filling line with a 20-minute changeover across 500 ml can format and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 8135–11635 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

Integration is where the seasoning weighing and filling line either becomes part of the seasoning and sauce packers production line or stays a machine beside it. Standard encoder interface, shared CIP, shared recipe storage — those three decisions are what separate the two outcomes.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A seasoning weighing and filling line with a 4-inch HMI and a 20-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

The seasoning weighing and filling line keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 8135 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the seasoning weighing and filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 1400 × 2400 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

What is the actual connected load of the seasoning weighing and filling line?

The reference build draws 12–17 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Can the seasoning weighing and filling line run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 500 ml can product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
