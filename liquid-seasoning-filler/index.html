Liquid Seasoning Filler

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

The difference between a workable liquid seasoning filler and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for seasoning and sauce packers, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

Between the tank and the cap there is exactly one place where the quantity is set, and that is the liquid seasoning filler. On this build the dose is repeatable to ±0.3 ml across 330 ml containers at 1282–1932 units per hour.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare a liquid seasoning filler on what happens in the second hour of a shift, not the first. That means 0.3 ml at 1282 units per hour on 330 ml product, with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and a 8-minute changeover for the next campaign.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This liquid seasoning filler already handles a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity beyond demand is not a return, it is a risk. Size the liquid seasoning filler to contracted volume for 330 ml product, then let the 1282–1932 units per hour figure and the 0.3 ml tolerance do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a liquid seasoning filler with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and 1282–1932 bottles per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a seasoning and sauce packers operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

How long does a format changeover take on this liquid seasoning filler?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change on a new 330 ml format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
