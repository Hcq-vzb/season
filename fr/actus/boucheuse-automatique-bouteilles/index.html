Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Boucheuse automatique pour bouteilles suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 2441–3441 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the Boucheuse automatique pour bouteilles handle a 330 ml product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What does the Boucheuse automatique pour bouteilles do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
