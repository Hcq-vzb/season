Curry Powder Packing Machine

Published 2026-11-11 · KIWL Machine

A curry powder packing machine for seasoning and sauce packers is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Because a curry powder packing machine handles a defined step for seasoning and sauce packers, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 12 oz product at 19364–23864 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

For a curry powder packing machine aimed at seasoning and sauce packers operations, the practical reference build is 304/316L stainless steel contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 13–18 kW connected load inside 1600 × 3600 × 2400 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a curry powder packing machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the seasoning and sauce packers production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 12-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the seasoning and sauce packers production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

The curry powder packing machine was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a seasoning and sauce packers operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 19364 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this curry powder packing machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

What spare parts should be held for the curry powder packing machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 19364 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How does the curry powder packing machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the curry powder packing machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
